General Instructions

for Parents and Students Attending School Events

Arrival Time

Please reach the school 15–30 minutes before the event start time to avoid last-minute rush.

Dress Code

  • Students must wear proper school uniform unless specified otherwise.
  • Parents should dress decently and modestly, keeping the school environment in mind.

ID Cards

Carry your school ID card (for students) or parent pass (if issued) for security reasons.

Seating and Discipline

  • Follow the seating arrangements as guided by the school staff.
  • Maintain silence and decorum during performances and speeches.

Mobile Phones

  • Keep mobile phones on silent mode.
  • Do not use flash photography or record videos unless permitted.

Parking

  • Park your vehicles only in the designated parking area.
  • Do not block the school gate or neighboring premises.

Food and Water

  • Avoid bringing outside food unless instructed.
  • Carry a water bottle if needed. Drinking water will also be available on campus.

Special Needs

If your child has any special needs, kindly inform the class teacher in advance.

Health & Safety

In case of any health issues or emergencies, contact the nearest teacher or school staff immediately.

Exit Instructions

  • Wait for official announcements before exiting the venue.
  • Do not crowd the gates or push while leaving.

Thank you for your cooperation. We look forward to your enthusiastic participation!
